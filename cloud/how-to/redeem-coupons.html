Redeem a coupon

Agent index: llms.txt

Redeem a coupon from Billing to activate its plan benefit for your workspace. You need an AtomicMemory account and an admin role in the workspace that receives the benefit. If you open a shared coupon link before creating an account, the code stays with you through signup and normal workspace setup. You still review and explicitly redeem the benefit afterward.

Redeem your code

  1. Sign in to AtomicMemory.
  2. Open the account menu in the sidebar and check the current name. If you belong to several workspaces, open Organization and select the one that should receive the benefit. Switching takes you to Projects, so do this before you open Billing.
  3. Open Billing, then select Have a coupon? Redeem it here.
  4. Enter your code in Coupon code and select Check code. Review the plan, duration, and workspace shown on the form.
  5. Select Redeem coupon. The benefit starts when redemption succeeds. Confirmation shows your plan and expiry date.
  6. Select Back to Billing to view the active benefit. Check Usage for your plan's allowance and current usage.

A shared coupon link sends you to sign-in if necessary, then opens the same redemption form with the code filled in. If you need an account, choose signup from sign-in and finish normal workspace setup. The app then returns you to the coupon form. Account setup does not claim the benefit. Check the displayed workspace before selecting Redeem coupon.

Check the expiry

Billing shows when your benefit ends. At expiry, your workspace returns to Basic and its limits apply. Your saved memories and projects remain in your account. Having many saved memories does not itself trigger deletion or a charge. If your current monthly usage exceeds Basic's allowance, metered operations can pause until the period resets or you choose another plan. New projects are blocked when you reach Basic's project limit. Existing projects are kept. Redemption requires no card and creates no payment subscription, automatic renewal, or charge. A timed plan coupon gives access to that plan's allowances; it does not add a separate cash or credit balance.

AtomicMemory emails the person who redeemed the coupon a reminder during the seven days before expiry, and a notice during the seven days after the benefit ends. A notice missed in that window is not sent later. Check Billing for the current expiry even if no email arrives; a missing email does not mean the benefit failed to end or was extended. Extending the benefit updates the notice schedule. Email delivery does not control your plan's expiry.

Keep your plan after the coupon

To stay on the coupon's plan after it ends, select Keep Pro (or Max or Scale) in Billing. Checkout asks for a card and shows your first charge date before you confirm. Eligible accounts aren't charged until the coupon's expiry date; if checkout shows a charge today, your account isn't eligible for that free period. Canceling later does not refund a charge that already happened. After a successful checkout the subscription replaces the coupon benefit, so canceling it does not restore the coupon. The expiry reminder email links to the same option.

Choosing a different paid plan starts billing immediately and ends the coupon benefit. Review the payment terms before confirming checkout. A paid subscription follows its own billing terms; coupon expiry does not cancel it or change it to Basic. Coupon redemption itself never starts paid billing.

Add another coupon

You can redeem different codes for the same plan while its benefit is active. The new coupon adds its months to your current expiry and keeps your remaining time. For example, if Pro ends on March 8, a new two-month Pro coupon extends it to May 8. Billing shows the updated expiry after redemption.

Each code works once per workspace, and you can use it in only one workspace. Retrying the latest successful redemption does not add more time. A coupon for a different plan can be redeemed after the active benefit ends. An expired benefit does not reduce the duration of a new coupon; that benefit starts on redemption.

Resolve a redemption problem

  • Invalid or unavailable code: Check the spelling and select Check code again. The code may have expired, been disabled, or reached its redemption limit.
  • Not a workspace admin: Ask an admin of the intended workspace to redeem it.
  • Paid subscription or Polar trial: Contact support from the app to review eligibility. A coupon does not replace an existing paid subscription or its trial. A Basic subscription through Polar does not prevent redemption.
  • Already claimed: Retrying a successful claim in the same workspace keeps its original expiry; it does not extend it.
  • Claimed in another workspace: You can use a code in only one workspace. If you already redeemed it elsewhere, it can't be applied to a second one.
  • Different plan from your active benefit: Wait for the current benefit to end before redeeming that code. Coupons for different plans do not overlap.

If you edit a checked code, select Check code again before redeeming.
